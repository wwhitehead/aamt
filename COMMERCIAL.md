# Commercial licensing

`.aamt` is free for individuals, non-commercial research, a 60-day evaluation and small teams (LICENSE.md,
Section 1). Everything else is licensed. Contact **licensing@asamanthinks.com**; pricing is quoted on request.

| Licence | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Developer** | Teams using `.aamt` code or the `aamt` runtime in their own products and internal production systems | Per-seat, annual. Commercial use rights for the code and runtime, patent licence for that use, updates. |
| **Enterprise** | On-premises, air-gapped and regulated deployments | Per deployment, annual. Everything in Developer, plus indemnity, security patches and an SLA with named support. |
| **OEM / Platform** | Embedding `.aamt` in hardware, an operating system, a device or another product; redistribution; serving to third parties | Upfront fee plus a per-unit royalty or a revenue share. Redistribution rights and co-marketing. |
| **BeyondSight models** | Production use of the BeyondSight models | Per deployment or per active user, separate from the runtime. Custom fine-tunes and domain experts available. |
| **AAMT Compatible certification** | Other runtimes and tools that read or write `.aamt` | Conformance test suite, the right to use the "AAMT Compatible" mark, and a listing. Annual. |

## What counts as commercial use

Bright lines, so nobody has to guess (the licence text governs):

- your organisation crossed **US $1M revenue**, **US $5M raised**, or **25 people**; or
- you charge anyone for a product or service that uses `.aamt` code, the runtime or the models; or
- you host, serve or convert `.aamt` for anyone outside your organisation; or
- you build a competing runtime, converter or model-distribution product; or
- you embed `.aamt` in a device or another product, or redistribute it.

## Startups

Below the thresholds, register for free at asamanthinks.com/licensing and build. When you cross one you have 60
days to move to a commercial licence, with pricing that grows with you.
